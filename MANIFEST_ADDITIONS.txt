Add the following entries inside the <application> tag in
android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml:

NOTE: BootReceiver is already declared in the manifest. Update it to also
handle QUICKBOOT_POWERON if desired, and add NotificationReceiver and the
Glance widget receiver as shown below.

<!-- Notification Receiver (triggered by BootReceiver alarm) -->
<receiver android:name=".core.receivers.NotificationReceiver" android:exported="false"/>

<!-- Glance App Widget -->
<receiver android:name=".widget.SpendMindWidgetReceiver" android:exported="true">
    <intent-filter>
        <action android:name="android.appwidget.action.APPWIDGET_UPDATE"/>
    </intent-filter>
    <meta-data
        android:name="android.appwidget.provider"
        android:resource="@xml/spend_mind_widget_info"/>
</receiver>
